How the model works

A regularized logistic model predicts settlement from the market midpoint, three-level book imbalance, spread, and time remaining. It has no independent commodity price feed or language-model service. The assistant’s explanations are rule based.

Simulation rules

Per-window maximum loss$25
Minimum remaining edge2¢ / contract
Uncertainty deduction5¢ / contract
Slippage charge0.5¢ / contract
Depth participation≤25% of top level

The 5¢ deduction is a fixed research parameter, not a confidence interval. Hold positions until a finalized exchange outcome. Wins do not replenish the daily loss allowance. Day boundary: America/New_York.

Activity

Reads public production quotes; all fills and balances exist only in this simulator. Uses the repository's KalshiAPI.create_order with a local paper transport. No exchange order requests are sent. Keep the app running and computer/Codespace awake to collect data. Polling can miss price moves; displayed depth does not guarantee a fill. Data documentation · Fee schedule
